# Local services marketplace in Montenegro: competitive landscape

**Caveats.** My web search runs from the US, so the search results below may not match what Google shows in Montenegro. Treat them as a signal and check them with a Montenegrin IP or a rank tool before you rely on them. I also couldn't get traffic numbers or funding for any Montenegrin competitor.

**Bottom line:** Montenegro has several small "find a tradesman" sites, most launched recently, and a few single-vertical tools (beauty booking, tutoring). No site covers all categories the way kosnica.net does, with psychologists, tutors, beauty, cleaning and trades. kosnica.net does not cover Montenegro. Search results for local service queries are weak: they show exact-match one-person sites, old template directories and Facebook pages.

## 2. International and regional sites worth learning from

- **kosnica.net (Serbia).** 171 categories, 152 cities, 3,199 businesses. Profiles show a "responds in 15 min" badge, job counts ("500+"), and a live feed of recent reviews. Price guides at `/cena/<service>` (e.g. [čuvanje dece](https://kosnica.net/cena/cuvanje-dece): average 925 RSD/h, range 600–1,000, top providers, FAQ, "Do you offer services?" call to action). Pages by service and city like `/peglanje-beograd`. Contact is by in-app chat or a phone call; there's no booking. Separate "Košnica Biz" app for providers. Basic profiles are free; visibility and sponsored placement are paid (prices not published). [kosnica.net](https://kosnica.net/)
- **Thumbtack.** Instant Match pairs a customer with pros using the pros' preferences (jobs, area, availability, budget). Public [cost estimate pages](https://www.thumbtack.com/prices). Top Pro badge based on response rate within 1 hour. Pros pay per qualifying lead. [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/thumbtack)
- **Bark.com.** Pros buy credits and spend 5–20 credits per lead (UK ~£1.20 per credit, roughly £7–40 per lead). An "Elite Pro" subscription adds 5 free leads per month and 20% off credits. Credits bought since November 2025 expire after 3 months, which pros complain about. [Bark pricing](https://www.bark.com/en/us/sellers/pricing/), [NobleLeads](https://nobleleads.uk/blog/how-much-do-bark-credits-cost)
- **Checkatrade.** Subscription tiers: Free £0, Approved £30/month, Growth from £59/month. The "Checkatrade Vetted" scheme checks ID, qualifications and insurance, and that trust badge is what it sells. [localadder](https://localadder.co.uk/checkatrade-cost/)
- **Treatwell and Booksy (beauty).** Treatwell charges a subscription plus 35% commission on a new client's first booking. Booksy charges about £40/month SaaS, with optional "Boost" at 30% of a new client's first visit and 0% after that. Lesson: charge for new clients, not for repeat ones. [Booksy vs Treatwell](https://biz.booksy.com/en-gb/comparison/treatwell-comparison)
- **Fixly (Poland, OLX Group).** Pay per lead at 15–80 PLN per contact, whether or not it turns into a job. Customers post a request and get quotes. Strong app for providers. [Oferia](https://oferia.com.pl/pl/blog/fixly-opinie-prowizje-cennik-porownanie-oferia-2026)
- **MyHammer (Germany).** Membership plus paid contacts, roughly €5–25 for simple jobs and €40–90+ for complex ones. It is criticized for charging for leads that don't convert ([WiWo](https://www.wiwo.de/erfolg/gruender/handwerksportale-in-der-kritik-myhammer-interessiert-nur-der-eigene-profit/28073252.html)).
- **Trebam.hr (Croatia).** One request gets up to 6 offers; it claims 5,000+ active projects a month. Publishes price lists, e.g. [apartment cleaning prices](https://www.trebam.hr/cijene/servisi-za-ciscenje/ciscenje-apartmana-cijena), which is the model for Montenegrin coastal cleaning.
- **Njuškalo and Halo Oglasi.** Classifieds with service categories ([Halo majstori](https://www.halooglasi.com/oglasi/majstori)). Their strength is audience, not trust. Revenue comes from promoted ads.
- **Serbian tradesmen sites.** [moj-majstor.rs](https://www.moj-majstor.rs/), [dobrimajstori.rs](https://www.dobrimajstori.rs/majstori) (map and reviews), [e-majstori.rs](https://e-majstori.rs/), [pronadjimajstora.rs](https://pronadjimajstora.rs/) (request and offers).
- I didn't look into Houzz or Oferteo individually. From general knowledge: Houzz sells "Pro+" subscriptions for local ad placement, and Oferteo is a Polish request-for-quote site where companies pay per contact. Neither is verified here.

**Features that make the best ones work:**

1. A short request form with photos and a timing field ("hitno / ove sedmice / fleksibilno": urgent / this week / flexible).
2. Match 3–5 pros and cap the number, instead of broadcasting to everyone.
3. Reviews only from verified jobs.
4. Response-time and "Top" badges.
5. Public price guides that also bring search traffic.
6. Instant booking for beauty and therapy, where appointments are time slots.
7. A separate app for providers with push notifications for new leads.

## 3. Montenegro specifics

**The 25 municipalities, 2023 census** (Monstat via [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Montenegro)). Figures are mostly municipality totals. The table mixes town and municipality data in places, noted in the list.

Podgorica 179,505 · Nikšić 65,705 · Bar 45,812 · Bijelo Polje 38,662 · Herceg Novi 30,824 · Budva ~27,400 (fastest growing since 2011) · Berane 24,645 · Pljevlja 24,134 · Rožaje 23,184 · Kotor 22,746 · Danilovgrad 18,617 · Tivat 16,338 · Cetinje 14,494 · Tuzi 12,979 · Ulcinj 11,215 (this looks like the town, not the municipality, which is about 20k, so check it) · Plav 9,060 · Mojkovac 6,728 · Kolašin 6,700 · Petnjica 4,957 · Andrijevica 3,971 · Gusinje 3,933 · Žabljak 2,941 · Zeta (seat Golubovci; the table gives 2,521 for the town, the municipality is about 16k, unverified) · Plužine 2,177 · Šavnik 1,569.

Newer municipalities: Petnjica (2013), Gusinje (2014), Tuzi (2018), Zeta (2022). Podgorica, Nikšić, Bar and Budva together hold 52.9% of the population. For search, use settlements as well as municipalities: Bečići, Rafailovići, Petrovac, Sveti Stefan, Igalo, Dobrota, Prčanj, Sutomore, Golubovci, Spuž.

**Language.** Most sites use Latin script and ijekavica:

- cijena / cjenovnik, mjesto, vrijeme, ocjena, zahtjev, provjeren majstor, savjet, čuvanje djece, lijepo, sedmica (week)
- but "uslovi korišćenja" (terms of use) and "usluge" stay as in Serbian
- colloquial "haus majstor" and "kućni majstor" (handyman) are both common.

Other languages matter too: Albanian in Ulcinj, Tuzi, Plav and Gusinje, and English, Russian, Ukrainian and Turkish for the large expat and property-owner groups on the coast. Tourists from Russia (16.4% of overnight stays), Ukraine and Turkey show how big these communities are. No competitor offers English or Russian pages except elektricar24 (English).

**Currency.** EUR, used unilaterally. No currency conversion is needed, and prices can be compared directly with Croatian ones.

**Card payments:**

- **Stripe** does not accept Montenegrin companies ([stripe.com/global](https://stripe.com/global)). You'd need a company in a supported country, such as Croatia, Slovenia, an EU state or the US.
- **PayPal** lets Montenegrin users send money but not receive it (Central Bank of Montenegro, 2023: [montenegrobusiness.eu](https://montenegrobusiness.eu/paypal-can-start-receiving-money-immediately/)).
- **Local options:**
  - [Monri / WSPay](https://monri.com/) and **AllSecure** are the payment gateways.
  - Acquiring banks: **Hipotekarna banka** (€25/month if turnover is low, up to 4% per transaction), **CKB** (now OTP group, through ACI, WSPay or AllSecure) and **Zapad banka** ([ecommerce4all.me](https://ecommerce4all.me/en/module/e-payment/offers-and-possibilities/)).
  - [Payten](https://www.payten.com/en/offers/for-merchants/e-commerce/webpay-payment-gateway-monri/) resells Monri.
  - NLB and Erste probably also offer acquiring, but I didn't verify that.
- **Paddle and Lemon Squeezy** are merchant-of-record services built for software. Whether they would accept marketplace lead credits, and whether they pay out to Montenegro, is unconfirmed; check their acceptable-use terms.
- **Suggested setup:** Monri plus a local bank. Keep bank transfer (uplatnica) and invoices as a fallback for providers. Card payments by Montenegrin companies must also comply with Montenegro's fiscal receipt law (e-fiscalization, EFI).

**Domain.** .me is run by doMEn ([domen.me](https://domen.me)). There are no residency rules and it costs about $8–12 a year. Every local competitor uses .me, so a short brandable name matters. Consider also registering the English words (e.g. "majstor.me"-style names), which are probably already taken.

**Seasonality.** 2025 set a record: 2.73M tourist arrivals and 15.4M overnight stays, 92.6% of them at the coast ([Monstat](https://www.monstat.org/eng/novosti.php?id=4312)). This drives demand from April to October on the coast for:

- apartment turnover cleaning, linen and laundry
- AC servicing (klima servis) in spring
- pool maintenance, pest control, small repairs between guests
- photographers and makeup artists for weddings
- boat and yacht cleaning (Citymaid offers this)
- property management

Off-season demand shifts to renovations (adaptacije, which run October to April on the coast), heating and boilers in the north (Pljevlja, Žabljak, Kolašin), tutoring during the school year, and psychologists all year.

**Most-demanded services** (inferred from which categories competitors list and which search results appear; not measured): plumber, electrician, AC servicing, painter/drywall, tiler, handyman, cleaning and deep cleaning, moving, appliance repair, carpenter/kitchen fitting, locksmith/metalwork, plus tutors (maths, English), psychologists, hairdressers, beauticians and nails, massage.

## 4. SEO opportunities

Query patterns look like "vodoinstalater podgorica", "električar budva", "čišćenje apartmana budva", "psiholog podgorica", "klima servis bar", "privatni časovi matematike podgorica", "moler nikšić", "dubinsko pranje namještaja budva", "hitni vodoinstalater", "cijena krečenja po m2", plus English queries like "plumber budva" and "cleaning service kotor".

**Who ranks now (US-based results):**

- "vodoinstalater podgorica": exact-match domains (vodoinstalaterpodgorica.org, vodoinstalater.co.me), Facebook, biznissajt.me and mojbiznis.me templates.
- "električar budva": planplus.rs (1 listing), elektricar24.me, elektricari.me.
- "psiholog podgorica": Facebook, a forum and small single-person sites.

All of these are weak, low-quality results. brzimajstor.me is the only one building proper trade-by-city pages.

**Tactics:**

1. Build pages for each service × city: about 25 municipalities plus 10–15 coastal settlements × about 100 services. Only publish (let Google index) a page once it has at least 3 real providers, so you don't get penalized for thin content.
2. Publish price guides like kosnica's /cena/ pages, e.g. "Cijena krečenja u Crnoj Gori 2026: X €/m²". Base them on quotes from the platform and put a "od X €" (from X €) snippet in the page title, as SrediMe does.
3. Add structured data: LocalBusiness and Service schema, AggregateRating and FAQ.
4. Get backlinks from Montenegrin news portals (Vijesti, CdM, RTCG) with data stories such as "Koliko koštaju majstori u 2026" (what tradesmen cost in 2026).
5. Create a Google Business Profile for the platform, and help providers claim their own profiles as an onboarding perk.
6. Publish English and Russian versions of the coastal pages.
7. Write seasonal content before each season: "klima servis prije sezone" (AC service before the season), apartment-cleaning checklists.
8. Pull in providers by importing public listings from biznissajt and oglasi and letting businesses claim their profile, only if that's legally clean.

## 5. How a Montenegrin version could beat kosnica.net and stay simple

1. **Two ways to use it.** Search the directory and call directly (like kosnica), or send one request and get up to 3–5 quotes (like brzimajstor, Thumbtack or Trebam). Capping the number protects pros from wasted leads.
2. **Viber and WhatsApp first.** Add click-to-Viber and click-to-WhatsApp buttons and send lead alerts on Viber. Viber is dominant in Montenegro, and many pros won't install another app. (This is based on regional habits, not data.)
3. **Trust as the main selling point**, aimed at the "scam tradesmen" fear:
   - reviews only from verified jobs (the customer confirms, as on meshtar)
   - ID and registered-business check against the tax authority's business register
   - a "Provjeren" (verified) badge
   - response-time badges
   - no deleting of negative reviews, with a right to reply
4. **Prices up front.** Profiles show "od X €" and hourly or per-m² prices. Add a national price-guide page for each service.
5. **A coastal / hosts product:**
   - English and Russian interface
   - recurring turnover-cleaning bookings with calendar sync (import from Airbnb and Booking)
   - a "property manager" category
   - seasonal home-page modules (AC servicing in April and May, heating in October)
6. **Instant booking only where it makes sense:** time slots for beauty, psychologists and tutors (including online sessions, which suit small municipalities). Trades stay request-a-quote.
7. **Monetization designed to feel fair:**
   - free basic profile
   - optional "Pro" subscription (e.g. €9–19/month: top placement in the city, badge, stats)
   - pay-per-lead credits at €1–5 a lead (Montenegrin price levels), with automatic refunds for fake or unreachable leads and credits that don't expire (the opposite of Bark's 3-month rule)
   - beauty and therapy: first-booking commission only, like Booksy Boost
   - payments through Monri and bank transfer, with fiscal receipts issued as the law requires
8. **Signup in under 60 seconds** for providers: phone number plus SMS or Viber code, pick services and cities from a list, add photos. Let pros set the radius or towns they cover (e.g. "Podgorica + Danilovgrad + Tuzi").
9. **Cover all 25 municipalities from day one** in the city picker, and group them into three regions (Primorje, Centar, Sjever) so thin areas still show results, e.g. "Nema u Plavu, prikazujemo Berane i Andrijevicu" (none in Plav, showing Berane and Andrijevica).
10. **Easy for customers:**
    - no account needed to call
    - request by photo or voice note
    - a "Hitno" (urgent, 24/7) filter
    - a map (like meshtar)
    - native ijekavica copy, plus Albanian toggles for Ulcinj, Tuzi, Plav and Gusinje
11. **Supply before demand:** get 5+ providers per category in the 6 biggest cities (Podgorica, Nikšić, Bar, Budva, Herceg Novi, Kotor/Tivat) before spending on search traffic. Every current competitor fails here (brzimajstor shows only 80+ pros in total).
